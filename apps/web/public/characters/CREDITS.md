The files are shipped unmodified. Each player gets one of the three looks, picked from their player id (`characterAsset.ts` `variantFor`); the kit's "Enemy" is the red-helmet person, shown as "Red helmet". At runtime the client keeps one of the 14 weapons
attached to the right hand (RocketLauncher, the kit's chunky toy-like tube) and tints it foam orange, so it reads as a
foam blaster rather than a realistic gun (AGENTS.md section 2). The animations
are the pack's own; nothing is retargeted (AGENTS.md section 5).
